## 第一张：封面主图 / FRONT COVER

### 设计要求 / Design Brief

一颗悬浮的星球，半边是水墨风格的山水意境（东方），半边是发光电路纹理（西方/科技），两半在星球中央交汇成太极阴阳鱼。太极的阴阳鱼眼，变成两个等亮度的小光球——一个代表"生产"，一个代表"分配"。从星球中心向外辐射的光线，连接着星球表面的每一个节点。所有节点亮度相等，没有中心，没有特权。一层几乎透明的金色光环，环绕着整个星球（代表人类命运共同体）。

A floating spherical planet, left half in Eastern ink-wash style, right half in luminous circuit aesthetics. The two halves merge in the center into a perfect Taiji symbol. The "fish eyes" become two equally glowing orbs — one for "Production," one for "Distribution." Light rays connect every node equally. A subtle golden halo surrounds the planet (Community of Shared Future).

### 英文提示词 / English Prompt

```
Design the FRONT COVER of a philosophical book: "The AI Epoch — When Machines Learn to Distribute."

Central Image: A floating spherical planet (portrait orientation, 6x9 aspect ratio).

LEFT HALF — Eastern/Classical aesthetic (Yin):
- Traditional Chinese ink wash painting style
- Abstract mountain silhouettes in deep indigo (#1a1a3e) and midnight black (#0d0d1a)
- Subtle calligraphic brush strokes forming cloud patterns
- Classical Chinese lattice window patterns as texture
- Faint silhouette of ancient Chinese architecture in mist
- Color palette: deep indigo, midnight black, silver mist (#c0c0d0)

RIGHT HALF — Western/Technology aesthetic (Yang):
- Glowing circuit board patterns in electric cyan (#00f0ff) and neon blue (#0066ff)
- Flowing binary code (0s and 1s) as abstract luminous lines
- Neural network nodes connected by glowing threads
- Abstract geometric shapes suggesting AI circuitry
- Faint outline of a robotic mechanical arm made of light
- Color palette: electric cyan (#00e5ff), neon blue (#0066ff), pure white (#ffffff)

CENTER JUNCTION — Taiji Fusion:
- A perfect Taiji (yin-yang) symbol where the two halves meet at the planet's equator
- The "fish eyes" are two small glowing orbs, equal brightness
- One orb contains a tiny gear icon (Production)
- One orb contains an even-split symbol like a balance (Distribution)
- Warm golden light rays emanate from the center outward

HUMAN ELEMENT:
- Above the planet: 5-7 abstract human silhouettes in simple geometric forms
- All silhouettes are the same height, same size, same subtle glow
- Thin luminous threads connect each figure to different nodes on the planet
- This represents equality — everyone connected, everyone equal

COMMUNITY HALO:
- A very subtle, barely-visible golden (#ffd700) circular halo surrounds the entire planet
- So subtle it feels like an afterimage — not a heavy ring, just a warm glow at the edges
- This represents "Community of Shared Future for Mankind"

BACKGROUND:
- Deep space black (#000000) fading to deep purple (#1a0a2e) at top
- Subtle star field with abstract constellation patterns
- Gradient from black (bottom) to deep space indigo (#0f0f2d)

BOTTOM TEXT:
- "THE AI EPOCH" in modern sans-serif metallic silver font, thin weight
- "When Machines Learn to Distribute" in elegant thin serif font below, silver
- At the very bottom center: small "CGHub" logo text

COLOR PALETTE:
- Primary background: Deep space indigo (#0f0f2d), midnight blue (#1a1a4d)
- Eastern elements: Deep indigo (#1a1a3e), midnight black (#0d0d1a), silver mist (#c0c0d0)
- Western elements: Electric cyan (#00e5ff), neon blue (#0066ff), white (#ffffff)
- Accent: Warm gold (#ffd700)
- Text: Metallic silver (#c0c0c0)

STYLE: Abstract, conceptual, minimalist but layered
MOOD: Serious, philosophical, futuristic yet timeless
TECHNICAL: 300 DPI, portrait 6x9 inches, RGB
OUTPUT: PNG with transparent background
```

### 意境描述（中文）/ Visual Description (Chinese)

```
封面视觉概念：《当机器学会分配》

核心星球：
一颗悬浮的星球，承载着人类文明的全部元素。星球的左半球是东方古典的意境——
水墨山水的朦胧美，古典窗格的秩序美，毛笔书写的流动美。右半球则是AI时代
的科技美学——发光的电路纹理，二进制数字的流动，神经网络节点的连接。

太极融合：
两半在星球中央交汇，形成完美的太极图。太极的阴阳鱼眼，变成两个等亮度
的小光球——一个代表"生产"，一个代表"分配"。这两个概念终于在技术时代
实现了统一：机器生产，机器分配。

平等之网：
从星球中心向外辐射的光线，连接着星球表面的每一个节点。所有节点亮度相等，
没有中心，没有特权。每一个连接到网络的人，都获得均等的分配权。

人类命运共同体：
一层几乎透明的金色光环，环绕着整个星球。它若隐若现，代表人类命运共同体
不是一个强制性的框架，而是一种自然而然形成的共识——因为当分配规则被
写在代码里，人类终于可以真正"共同体"了。

色彩哲学：
深空蓝作为底色，代表AI时代的深邃与未知。金色点缀，代表古典智慧的光芒。
电路蓝代表科技，银白代表光明与平等。整体色调沉稳厚重，但不沉闷——因为
有光在流动。
```

## 第二张：封底 / BACK COVER

### 设计要求 / Design Brief

封底的视觉核心是一幅抽象信息图，解释"机器如何学会分配"——用三层对比的视觉语言：顶层是倒置的树（传统分配的批判），中层是去中心化网格（区块链方案），底层是站在等亮节点上的人类轮廓（AI赋能）。底部东西方各一句经典语录。

### 英文提示词 / English Prompt

```
Design the BACK COVER of the same book: "The AI Epoch — When Machines Learn to Distribute."

This is a philosophical visual essay explaining "How Machines Learn to Distribute."

THREE HORIZONTAL LAYERS (stacked vertically, top to bottom):

LAYER 1 — Critique of Traditional Distribution (Top):
- An INVERTED TREE visualization — roots pointing upward into dark sky, branches pointing downward
- A few large glowing orbs clustered at the top (the powerful: executives, platforms, capital)
- Countless tiny dim dots scattered at the bottom (ordinary people)
- Tree trunk is twisted and dark
- Color: deep crimson (#8b0000) fading to black, oppressive atmosphere
- Visual metaphor: wealth flows up, not down
- NO text labels — pure visual symbolism

LAYER 2 — Blockchain Solution (Middle):
- A DECENTRALIZED MESH GRID filling the space
- All nodes are the same size, same brightness
- Thin luminous cyan (#00e5ff) lines connecting every node to multiple neighbors
- NO central node — the network has no center
- Some nodes have small icons: gear (labor), lightbulb (innovation), coin (reward)
- Color: electric cyan (#00e5ff) on transparent/very dark background, ethereal glow
- Visual metaphor: transparent, equal, unbreakable

LAYER 3 — AI Empowerment (Bottom):
- Abstract human silhouettes (simple circle head + geometric body shapes) standing on/above the nodes
- All silhouettes are the same height, same size, same subtle golden glow (#ffd700)
- Thin silver (#c0c0c0) threads connecting all silhouettes to each other
- Together they form a circle of equals — a community
- Color: warm gold (#ffd700) silhouettes, silver (#c0c0c0) threads
- Very subtle warm glow at the very bottom edge (dawn/new beginning metaphor)

QUOTE SECTION (bottom strip, above publisher info):

LEFT SIDE — Western Quote:
"Knowledge is power."
— Francis Bacon
Small elegant serif font, silver color (#c0c0c0)

RIGHT SIDE — Eastern Quote:
"大道之行也，天下为公。" —《礼记》
Small elegant typography with Chinese aesthetic, gold color (#ffd700)
English translation below in tiny italic silver: "When the Great Way prevails, the world belongs to all."

BOTTOM CENTER:
Small "CGHub" logo
Tiny text: "Ignite Passion, Illuminate Dreams" in silver italic

BACKGROUND:
- Similar deep space gradient as front cover (black to deep indigo)
- Very subtle hexagonal grid pattern (blockchain motif) in extremely low opacity
- Much quieter and darker than front — this is a reflective, resting space

COLOR PALETTE:
- Primary: Deep space indigo (#0f0f2d)
- Layer 1: Deep crimson (#8b0000) to black
- Layer 2: Electric cyan (#00e5ff)
- Layer 3: Warm gold (#ffd700), silver (#c0c0c0)
- Text: Silver (#c0c0c0)

STYLE: Informational yet artistic, visual essay, layered meaning
MOOD: Analytical, hopeful, resolving
TECHNICAL: 300 DPI, portrait 6x9 inches, RGB
OUTPUT: PNG with transparent background
```

## 第三张：书脊 / SPINE

### 英文提示词 / English Prompt

```
Design the BOOK SPINE for the same book.

Spine orientation: Vertical (height matches book height, width = spine thickness)

TOP to BOTTOM gradient:
- Top: Deep space indigo (#0f0f2d)
- Middle: Transition from indigo to warm gold (#ffd700)
- Bottom: Pure warm gold (#ffd700)

CENTRAL ELEMENT:
- A single vertical luminous thread (cyan #00e5ff) running from top to bottom
- Along the thread: small evenly-spaced glowing dots (nodes)
- The thread has NO center — the dots are evenly distributed
- This represents decentralized distribution

ABOVE THE THREAD:
- "THE AI EPOCH" in small modern sans-serif font, vertical orientation, metallic silver

BELOW THE THREAD:
- Small "CGHub" logo in the center

TECHNICAL NOTES:
- Spine width: approximately 1.5-2cm (adjust based on actual book thickness)
- If the spine is too narrow for text, just use the thread and node pattern
- 300 DPI, RGB
- Output: PNG with transparent background
```

## 重要设计原则 / Key Design Principles

1. **不对抗**：不批评任何国家、文化或技术，而是融合东西方
2. **不炫耀**：不花哨，不商业感，要沉稳有深度
3. **有光感**：科技是光，智慧是光，分配公平也是光
4. **有层次**：第一眼看到星球，第二眼看到太极，第三眼看到人类命运共同体
5. **全球化**：面向中国和海外读者，视觉语言要跨文化
