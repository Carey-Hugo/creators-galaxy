# Creators Galaxy Hub(CGHub) book plan

## Recommended title

**After the One-Person Company: The Organization Revolution of the AI Era**

## Alternative titles

1. **Creators Galaxy Hub: How Super Individuals Build Together**
2. **From AI Productivity to Contribution Ownership**
3. **The New Organization Paradigm for the AI Era**
4. **Super Individuals Are Not Meant to Be Alone**

## Book positioning

This book explains why the rise of AI-powered one-person companies does not eliminate the need for organization. It creates the need for a new kind of organization.

Traditional companies were designed for a world where production required capital, offices, managers, and employees. The AI era changes that. Individuals can now use AI as digital labor, code as leverage, and media as distribution. But they still need trust, collaboration, contribution records, and fair allocation.

The book uses Creators Galaxy Hub(CGHub) as a practical case for exploring this shift.

## Target readers

- AI creators and independent builders.
- People exploring one-person companies or personal IP businesses.
- Web3 builders interested in real-world contribution ownership.
- Community operators and knowledge entrepreneurs.
- Ordinary people looking for a realistic entry point into AI-era entrepreneurship.

## Core argument

AI gives individuals the power to create. A new organization paradigm gives that power the ability to scale.

A one-person company is not the end of organization. It is the beginning of a more flexible, transparent, and contribution-based organization model.

## Proposed structure

### Part 1: The rise of the super individual

- Why AI changes the cost of creation.
- How digital labor, AI coding, and self media become new leverage.
- Why ordinary people have a better chance than before.

### Part 2: The limits of working alone

- Why productivity does not equal market success.
- Trust, distribution, and collaboration as hard bottlenecks.
- The loneliness and fragility of isolated creators.

### Part 3: The new organization paradigm

- From employment to co-creation.
- From platform capture to contribution ownership.
- From centralized allocation to transparent rules.
- Why DAO thinking matters even before full decentralization.

### Part 4: CGHub as a living experiment

- The mission and positioning of CGHub.
- Early tracks: local life, pet economy, sports social, idle sharing, knowledge community, AI tools.
- The community funnel: content, low-cost product, paid community, project groups, contribution records.

### Part 5: How ordinary people can participate

- Finding your role.
- Starting with one contribution.
- Building public trust.
- Recording value.
- Growing from participant to co-builder.

## Tone

The book should be idealistic but not exaggerated. It should speak to ordinary people in plain language, while still offering serious insight into AI, Web3, and organization design.

It should not sound like a get-rich-quick manual. It should sound like a field guide for people who want to build in a new era.
