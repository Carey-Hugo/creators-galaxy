# Creators Galaxy Hub(CGHub) partner recruitment plan

## 1. Why recruit partners now

Creators Galaxy Hub(CGHub) is at the stage where the most valuable people are not passive users, but early co-builders.

The project needs people who can help turn the idea into visible momentum: content, community, product experiments, local use cases, business development, and operational discipline.

This is not a recruitment campaign for employees. It is a call for partners who understand that the AI era rewards people who can create, coordinate, and compound trust.

## 2. Who we are looking for

CGHub is looking for people with at least one of the following strengths:

- content writing, short video, poster copy, or personal IP building;
- community operations and member activation;
- AI tool usage, automation, or agent workflow building;
- software development or product prototyping;
- local business resources and offline service experience;
- Web3, DAO governance, contribution tracking, or tokenomics knowledge;
- sales, partnerships, or project management.

The ideal early partner does not need to be perfect. The important thing is willingness to contribute, learn quickly, and work under transparent rules.

## 3. What partners can do

Partners can start from small but useful contributions:

1. Share CGHub content and bring in aligned members.
2. Help refine whitepapers, posters, and public articles.
3. Join a project track such as local life, pet economy, sports social, or idle sharing.
4. Build AI workflows that improve community productivity.
5. Organize topic discussions and collect user feedback.
6. Help define contribution records and future value allocation rules.

The principle is simple: start small, record clearly, compound over time.

## 4. Contribution ownership

CGHub's long-term direction is contribution ownership. This means that meaningful participation should not be invisible.

Early contributions may include:

- content creation;
- referrals and community growth;
- product research;
- design and development;
- operational support;
- business resources;
- governance proposals.

These contributions should be documented as the project matures. The goal is not to make vague promises, but to build a record that can support fairer recognition and future distribution.

## 5. Pricing and entry design

The current pricing logic is intentionally lightweight:

- Xiaobaotong content product: 10 RMB.
- Knowledge Planet community: 100 RMB.

The point is not to create a high barrier. The point is to filter for people who are willing to take the project seriously and participate rather than only observe.

## 6. Partner message

If you believe AI will create a new generation of super individuals, CGHub is a place to test that belief with action.

If you believe ordinary people should not only consume platforms but also help build and own them, CGHub is a place to explore that path.

If you are looking for a guaranteed return, this is not for you. If you are looking for a serious early-stage experiment where contribution matters, you are welcome to join.

## 7. Call to action

Join as an early partner of Creators Galaxy Hub(CGHub).

Bring one skill. Pick one project. Make one contribution. Let the record begin.
