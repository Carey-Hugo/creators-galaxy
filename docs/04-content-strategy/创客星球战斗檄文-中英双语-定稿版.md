# 檄文二：AI时代的三个杠杆，普通人翻身的机会
## Manifesto II: The Three Levers of the AI Era — Opportunities for Ordinary People to Rise

---

**中文正文：**

---

### 你有多少杠杆？

纳瓦尔在《纳瓦尔宝典》里说：

> "专长无法教授，但杠杆可以传递。"

什么是杠杆？

劳动力杠杆——让别人为你工作  
资本杠杆——让钱为你工作  
媒体杠杆——让代码/内容为你工作

过去，这三大杠杆只属于少数人。

你需要有钱才能投资，需要有人才能开公司，需要有渠道才能传播。

**普通人被杠杆挡在门外。**

---

### 但AI改变了一切。

**第一个杠杆：劳动力杠杆——AI数字员工**

7×24小时不眠不休的数字员工  
从"雇人"到"用AI"  
一个人 + AI = 一支军队

**第二个杠杆：代码杠杆——AI编程**

写代码不再是程序员的专利  
ChatGPT、Copilot让普通人也能开发产品  
从"想法"到"产品"的距离大大缩短

**第三个杠杆：媒体杠杆——自媒体爆发**

一个人可以触达数亿人  
短视频、直播、内容平台的黄金时代  
从普通人到IP的逆袭每天都在发生

---

### 第四个杠杆：资本杠杆——Web3新可能

为什么资本杠杆最难破？  
融资、股权、稀释——传统资本的门槛  
但Web3带来了新可能：通过代币实现资本杠杆

不是每个人都能拿到VC，但每个人都能参与到代币经济中  
不是少数人拥有股权，而是每个贡献者都能确权

---

**这是人类历史上第一次，普通人也能撬动如此巨大的杠杆。**

但光有杠杆还不够。

AI解决的是"做事"的问题  
但解决不了"成事"的问题

裂变需要人，传播需要信任  
网络效应需要多人参与

**一个人能做的事，有极限。**

---

### 这就是为什么我们需要组织。

不是传统的公司——那是工业时代的遗迹  
不是平台打工——那是中心化的剥削

我们需要一种**新的组织范式**：

> 每个人都是主人  
> 贡献即确权  
> 规则写入代码  
> 利益按劳分配

这不是乌托邦，这是我们正在用代码构建的现实。

---

**如果你也相信：**

AI时代，普通人有机会  
贡献者应该拥有价值  
这个世界的规则可以不一样

**那么，你就是我们要找的同路人。**

创客星球，正在招募。

---

**英文正文：**

---

### How Much Leverage Do You Have?

Naval Ravikant wrote in *The Almanack of Naval Ravikant*:

> "Specific knowledge cannot be taught, but it can be learned. Leverage cannot be taught, but it can be obtained."

What is leverage?

Labor leverage — making others work for you  
Capital leverage — making money work for you  
Media leverage — making code/content work for you

In the past, these three major levers belonged only to a select few.

You needed capital to invest, people to build companies, and channels to reach audiences.

**Ordinary people were locked out of leverage.**

---

### But AI Has Changed Everything.

**The First Lever: Labor Leverage — AI Digital Employees**

Digital employees working 24/7 without rest.  
From "hiring people" to "using AI."  
One person + AI = an entire army.

**The Second Lever: Code Leverage — AI Programming**

Coding is no longer the exclusive domain of programmers.  
ChatGPT and Copilot enable ordinary people to develop products.  
The distance from "idea" to "product" has drastically shortened.

**The Third Lever: Media Leverage — The Self-Media Explosion**

One person can reach hundreds of millions.  
The golden age of short videos, live streaming, and content platforms.  
Ordinary people becoming IPs happens every single day.

---

### The Fourth Lever: Capital Leverage — The New Possibilities of Web3

Why is capital leverage the hardest to break?  
Financing, equity, dilution — the barriers of traditional capital.  
But Web3 brings new possibilities: achieving capital leverage through tokens.

Not everyone can get VC funding, but everyone can participate in a token economy.  
Not a few holding equity, but every contributor can claim ownership.

---

**This is the first time in human history that ordinary people can wield such enormous leverage.**

But leverage alone isn't enough.

AI solves the problem of "getting things done."  
But it cannot solve the problem of "accomplishing something."

Growth requires people, dissemination requires trust,  
and network effects require collective participation.

**There is a ceiling to what one person can achieve alone.**

---

### This Is Why We Need Organization.

Not traditional companies — those are relics of the industrial age.  
Not platform labor — that is centralized exploitation.

We need a **new organizational paradigm**:

> Everyone is an owner.  
> Contribution is ownership.  
> Rules are written into code.  
> Benefits are distributed by labor.

This is not a utopia — this is the reality we are building in code.

---

**If you also believe:**

In the AI era, ordinary people have a chance.  
Contributors should own what they create.  
The world's rules can be different.

**Then you are the fellow traveler we are looking for.**

Creators Galaxy Hub is recruiting.
