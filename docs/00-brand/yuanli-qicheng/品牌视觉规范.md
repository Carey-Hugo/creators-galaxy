> **拍板日期**：2026-07-08

## 八、双语规范对外文案模板

**中文版（国内传播）**：

> **愿力启程（Intenpower Embark）**是面向 AI 时代普通人的教育品牌。它把"愿意一辈子做的事"（愿力）与 AI 工具、益邻家园实践结合，帮助每个人找到自己的方向，在躬身入局中长出属于自己的力量。
>
> 愿力不飘——是你内观看清自己后，发自内心充满喜悦地做自己想做的事。
> 启程不慌——是找一群志同道的同路人，一起上船远航。

**English Version（国际传播）**：

> **Intenpower Embark** is an education brand for ordinary people in the AI era. It bridges "what you're willing to do for a lifetime" (intentional power) with AI tools and the Elink Community practice, helping each person find their own direction and grow their strength through hands-on engagement.
>
> Intentional Power is not a fantasy—it is the inner clarity to do what your heart truly wants.
> To Embark is not to rush—it is to find fellow travelers and set sail together.
